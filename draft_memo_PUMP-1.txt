Subject: Spare-Parts Justification for Feed Pump 1 (PUMP-1)

Feed Pump 1 (PUMP-1) has a verified mean time between failures (MTBF) of 350.0 hours and a mean time to repair (MTTR) of 5.0 hours, producing an availability of 98.6%. Verified downtime cost to date totals $17,000, based on the reliability database's own metrics rather than estimation. Given this sustained cost exposure and the pump's role in feed operations, we recommend approving procurement of critical spare parts to shorten future repair cycles and limit additional downtime cost. This request falls under the manager-tier approval path, as the total cost remains below the $40,000 VP-tier guardrail threshold.
