Subject: Spare-Parts Justification — Feed Pump 1 (PUMP-1)

Feed Pump 1 (PUMP-1) has recorded a mean time between failures of 350.0 hours and a mean time to repair of 5.0 hours, yielding an availability of 98.6%. Downtime associated with this asset's failure history has cost the plant $17,000 to date, exceeding the $15,000 escalation threshold for spare-parts review. Given the repeated repair cycles and the recurring downtime cost, we recommend approving procurement of critical spare parts for PUMP-1 to reduce future MTTR and protect availability. We request manager sign-off to proceed with this purchase.
