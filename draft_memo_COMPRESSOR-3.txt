Subject: Spare-Parts Justification — Air Compressor 3 (COMPRESSOR-3)

Air Compressor 3 (COMPRESSOR-3) shows a mean time between failures of only 146.7 hours against a mean time to repair of 12.0 hours, pulling availability down to 92.4%. This failure pattern has driven total downtime cost to $43,200, well above the $15,000 escalation threshold and also above the $40,000 guardrail for elevated review. Given the frequency and duration of these outages, we recommend procuring critical spare parts for COMPRESSOR-3 to shorten repair cycles and restore availability. Because the cost exceeds the guardrail threshold, we request VP-tier approval to proceed.
