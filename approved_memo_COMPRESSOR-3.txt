Subject: Spare-Parts Justification — Air Compressor 3 (COMPRESSOR-3)

Air Compressor 3 (COMPRESSOR-3) has accumulated $43,200 in verified downtime cost, driven by a mean time between failures (MTBF) of 146.7 hours against a mean time to repair (MTTR) of 12.0 hours, yielding availability of only 92.4%. This is the highest cost exposure and lowest availability of any asset in this review, reflecting frequent and costly failures on a critical compressed-air system. We recommend approving procurement of spare parts to shorten repair cycles and limit further downtime cost. Because verified cost exceeds the $40,000 guardrail threshold, this request requires VP-tier approval.
